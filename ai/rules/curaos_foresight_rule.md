# Discovered work

Capture consequential debt, ideas, risks, research and prerequisites through
configured Rice task/knowledge authoring. Link the finding, affected project,
specification and required dependency to the current goal when it is needed to
achieve that goal. Unrelated future work belongs to a separate goal.

A hunch is not a ready task. Retrieve relevant evidence, write a bounded scope and
measurable acceptance, and verify the native blocker context. A foresight marker
is metadata, not permission to park required work. Complete ready dependencies
through the owning maintained workflow; preserve actual status and evidence.

Use the [configured maintained toolkit](../../docs/agents/rice-toolkit.md) and
project Rice skills. There is no template-local capture, sweep, issue store or
planning-file fallback. Keep credentials out of task documents and receipts.
